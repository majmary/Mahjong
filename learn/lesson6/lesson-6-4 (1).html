Exposures

What Makes a Valid Exposure

Exposures are created when a tile is called. To be a valid exposure, the group must be 3 or more tiles in length (no pairs or singles), and the tiles must be identical. This means that you cannot call an East wind tile to complete your NEWS group, nor can you call that tempting 4 Bam to complete your 4 5 6 7 run in Bams.

Open and Closed Hands

Some hands on the NMJL card must be completed without exposing tiles. These are marked by a C, for concealed, or closed. Others are marked with an x for exposed, where exposures are possible. The vast majority of hands on the NMJL card are open, or exposures allowed.

If you declare Mah Jong for a closed or concealed hand, and you have an exposure, you do not have Mah Jong after all, and your hand is dead. Play continues for the other players, but you cannot win that round.

Calling for Mah Jong

There are special rules if you are calling the last tile you need to complete Mah Jong. Because that tile will not be part of an exposure, you CAN call a tile that is part of a pair, a single tile, or even that East needed to complete your NEWS group.

And, you can call the last tile you need to complete a closed, or concealed hand.
