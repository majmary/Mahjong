Sorting & Passing

Sorting Your Tiles

When you get your tiles, sort them before you start passing. Most players sort by suit in numerical order, followed by dragons, winds, and jokers. Flowers are often placed on the far left of the rack. This sort roughly mirrors how hands are written on the NMJL card, which makes it easier to spot matches.

The First Charleston

The first pass is three tiles to the right (counterclockwise). All players choose three tiles to pass simultaneously, then slide them to the player on their right. Everyone receives three tiles at the same time.

The second pass is three tiles across — straight to the player sitting opposite you.

The third pass is three tiles to the left (clockwise). This is the final pass of the first Charleston, and it can be a blind pass — covered on the next page.

These three passes are called first right, first across, and first left. The names just indicate which Charleston round they belong to and which direction you're passing.

One Rule for All Passes

Jokers cannot be passed in the Charleston. Every other tile is a legal pass.
