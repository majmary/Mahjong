Strategy: Finding Your Hand

Now that you know the mechanics, let's talk strategy. How do you decide what to pass?

If you're lucky, your tiles will clearly point to a section on the card and the decision is easy — pass whatever doesn't fit. Most of the time, though, you'll need to work for it.

Step 1: Look for Power Groupings

Strategy

The first thing I do after sorting is scan for power groupings — sets of tiles, all in the same suit (or unsuited), that match patterns used on the card and are hard to build during play. If I have one, it's a strong signal to look at hands that feature it.

Power groupings to look for: the current year (2026 this year), 2468, 13579, NEWS, 369, and consecutive runs of 3 or more tiles in the same suit.

Once I find one, I go to the matching section on the card first — if my power grouping is NEWS, I start at Winds and Dragons — and then check any other hands on the card that use that combination.

Step 2: Look for Pairs, Pungs, and Kongs

Strategy

If no power grouping jumps out, I look for pairs (2 of a kind), pungs (3 of a kind), or kongs (4 of a kind). These are head starts toward many hands on the card.

If I have a kong, I look first at Quints and Any Like Numbers, then at 2468 or 13579 depending on whether the value is even or odd. Even without a joker, a kong makes a quint hand worth targeting.

When I have a pair, I look at what other tiles I have in that same suit and see if the combination gets close to a consecutive run, an even set, an odd set, or a multiple-of-three set.
