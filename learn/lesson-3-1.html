Set Up and Dealing

Of course, in my game all the set up and dealing is done for you. But you'll want to be playing in the real world too, so here's how to set things up.

What You Need

You'll need a table — relatively small and square is best, a card table works well. You'll need your set of tiles, and it makes things much easier if you have racks for all four players. You'll also need a pair of dice. That's it. You don't need fancy mats, special tables, or matching glassware — just a table and your Mah Jong set.

Setting Up

Put a rack in front of each player, one on each side of the table. The sloped side should face the player; the vertical side faces the middle of the table. Next, put all your tiles face down on the table and mix them thoroughly.

Building the Walls

"Building the walls" is just a fancy name for making an organized draw pile. Each player builds their own wall — a row of tiles, 19 long and 2 high, in front of their rack. If you don't have a rack, just make this row in front of you. Mah Jong tiles are wide enough to stand on their edges.

This is also a good opportunity to confirm you have a complete set. You should have exactly 152 tiles:

  • Bams, Cracks and Dots 1–9, four of each (108)
  • North, South, East and West winds, four of each (16)
  • Red, Green and White dragons, four of each (12)
  • Flowers (8)
  • Jokers (8)

If you have too many or too few tiles, stop and identify what's missing or extra before continuing.
