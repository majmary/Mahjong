Dealing

The Main Deal

Deal proceeds counterclockwise — to your right. East takes the first two stacks (4 tiles), then the player to East's right takes two stacks, then the player across, then the player to the left. Continue in this pattern until everyone has 12 tiles — that's three full rounds of dealing, 6 stacks per player.

When East's wall runs out: the player to East's left (clockwise) pushes their wall out to continue the deal. Dealing picks up from that wall without interruption.

The Final Tiles

Once everyone has 12 tiles, there's one final round where each player takes their last tile or tiles — and East takes two, ending up with 14 tiles total.
