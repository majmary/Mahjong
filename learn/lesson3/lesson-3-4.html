Finishing Up

Rack Your Tiles

Once all the tiles have been dealt, players look at their tiles and put them on their racks. If you don't have a rack, stand the tiles on their edges in a row in front of you — Mah Jong tiles are wide enough to stand. This is a good time to count your tiles: the dealer should have 14, everyone else should have 13.

Resetting the Wall

Remember those stacks East set aside at the beginning? The dealer now takes them and places them back in front of their rack. This mini-wall will be the last tiles drawn during the game, so they stay accessible throughout.

At this time, the dealer also passes the dice to the player on their right — this indicates who deals next round.

What Comes Next

With tiles dealt and the wall reset, it's time for everyone's favorite part of the game: the Charleston.

No exercise for this lesson — you'd need physical tiles. But if you have them handy, this is a great time to practice the full setup sequence from start to finish.
