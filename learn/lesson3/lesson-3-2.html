Determining East & Breaking the Wall

Determining Who is East

East is the dealer. If this is your first game in a session, each player rolls one die — the highest roll is East. If you have just finished a game, East is the player to the right of the last game's dealer (counterclockwise).

Breaking the Wall

East rolls both dice and counts that number of stacks from the right end of their own wall. This is called "breaking the wall."

Example: You are East and roll a 7. Starting from the right of your wall, count 7 stacks. Set those 7 stacks aside — you'll use them later. You now have 12 stacks remaining to deal from.

Those set-aside stacks will come back into play at the end — the dealer retrieves them after dealing is complete, and they become the last tiles drawn in the game.
