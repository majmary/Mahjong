So, you've decided to learn Mah Jong! I'm delighted to hear it. I remember back when I was just learning–all the vocabulary, all the rules, it was so overwhelming. First things first. Don't worry, you will be playing with confidence soon.

This tutorial is a step by step program, each a self contained lesson. Spend as long as you like on each step, revisiting them when necessary. The free tutorial has the basics–you can get going with just this information. The full tutorial (available with app purchase) includes comprehensive information at each step, and fun exercises to help you not just read about the information, but actually apply it. You can upgrade at any time. But it's totally doable with the free version too.

Mah Jong (there are lots of spellings, this is the one I'll use), has its roots in China, but has spread throughout the world and there are many different variations. This tutorial is for American Mah Jong–the dominant type among Americans of no particular ethnicity.
