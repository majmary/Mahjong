Tiles

American Mah Jong is played with 152 tiles. Tile sets are much like playing cards — they can come in all kinds of designs, but there are classic, traditional designs. If you are looking to buy a set, you need one that has:

  • 3 suits — Bams, Cracks and Dots in numbers 1–9. There are 4 of each tile. (108 tiles)
  • 4 winds — North, South, East and West. There are 4 of each. (16 tiles)
  • 3 dragons — Green, Red and White. There are 4 of each. (12 tiles)
  • 8 flowers — these sometimes come as 4 flowers and 4 seasons. (8 tiles)
  • 8 jokers (8 tiles)

You do NOT need animal tiles, red 5s, or blanks to play American Mah Jong.

In the following pages, I'll show you the traditional designs for all of the tiles, and the designs I used in my game to make things easier to read.
