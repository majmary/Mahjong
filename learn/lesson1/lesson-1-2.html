Bams & Cracks

Bams

Bams are short for Bamboo, and usually feature a stylized bamboo graphic. The 1 bam is always a bird of some kind, which can be easily confused with a flower (it's stylized, remember!) or a joker, so be careful. It's just a 1 bamboo.

In my game, the standard tiles are just numbers or letters paired with a color. The Bams are green — because bamboo.

Cracks

Crack is short for character, a reference to the black and red Chinese character illustration on the face of the tiles. These are the most straightforward to identify — there are no tricky confusions with other suits. They are, however, the hardest to differentiate within their suit, especially if you end up playing with a set that does not have Western numbers on it.

In my game, cracks are red — because of the red and black color scheme of the traditional tiles.
