Jokers & Other Tiles

Jokers

[ image: traditional joker tile ]

Jokers in American Mah Jong are wild cards — but wild cards with limits. Jokers cannot be used in sequences (like 1 2 3 4 5) or groups of non-identical tiles (like NEWS). They also cannot be used in a pair, or as a single tile. Jokers can only be used in sets of 3 or more identical tiles.

When we start exploring the legal hands in American Mah Jong, you will see that there are some hands that cannot use jokers at all!

In my game, jokers are yellow, with a J to denote their rank.

Tiles Not Used in American Mah Jong

  • Blanks — these are there to replace any lost tiles, or to be converted into tiles needed for other variations of Mah Jong.
  • Animals — needed for Singapore Mah Jong. They are typically a rooster, a centipede, a cat and a rat.
  • Red Fives — needed for Richi Mah Jong, a Japanese variation.
