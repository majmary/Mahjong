Non-Suited Tiles & the White Dragon

So far we've covered suited tiles — numbers and dragons. Now let's look at how the card represents tiles that don't belong to a suit.

Non-Suited Tile Notation

The non-suited tiles are represented on the card by these letters:

  • F — Flower
  • N, E, W, S — North, East, West, South winds
  • 0 — White Dragon (acting as a zero)

The White Dragon's Dual Role

The white dragon is uniquely versatile. It can act as either a suited dragon or a suitless zero, depending on what a hand needs.

When the card shows a 0, that is a white dragon acting as a zero. In this role it is suitless — it does not need to match the suit of the other tiles in the group. Any time you see a group like 2026, the suited tiles share a suit, and the white dragon fills the 0 position without a suit of its own.

When the card shows a D, a white dragon can fill that spot — but in this role it is a suited dragon, associated with the Dots suit. It must obey the suit requirements of the hand.

This means the white dragon is the only tile in the game that can play two completely different roles depending on what the hand requires. When you see a 0 on the card, reach for your white dragon. When you see a D, remember the white dragon is only valid if the Dots suit works for that group.
