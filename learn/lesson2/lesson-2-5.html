White Dragon in Practice

Let's put the white dragon's dual role to work with a real hand example:

2026  222  666  DDDD  (Any 3 suits)

The 2026 group is in green. The 2, 0, 2, and 6 are in Suit 1 — with the white dragon filling the 0 position as a suitless tile. The suited 2s and 6 all share Suit 1, and the white dragon sits among them without a suit.

The 222 and 666 groups are both red — Suit 2.

The DDDD group is blue — a third distinct suit (Suit 3), since D represents a suited dragon.

Here are two valid fills of this hand:

[ image: 2b WD 2b 6b 2c2c2c 6c6c6c WD WD WD WD ]
[ image: 2c WD 2c 6c 2d2d2d 6d6d6d GD GD GD GD ]

Notice that in the first example, one white dragon fills the suitless 0 role in the 2026 group, while four more white dragons fill the suited Dots dragon role in the DDDD group. Two different white dragon tiles, two different jobs — that's the dual role in action.
